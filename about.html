Meet the Founder

Working musicians and longtime teachers, hand-picked for patience as much as talent.

Vishnuvardhan Aditya

Founder & CEO

Music has always been more than a profession—it is my passion. For the past eight years, I have dedicated my career to teaching students from beginners to advanced musicians while also working professionally as a Music Director and Feature Film Composer. Harmony Music Academy was founded with one simple vision: To provide world-class music education that is affordable, engaging, and personalized for every student. Today, students from India and overseas learn with us through structured one-on-one sessions that combine classical fundamentals with modern music education.
